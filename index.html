Privacy Policy

Merge Kitchen - Last updated: May 13, 2025

This Privacy Policy describes how Engin Kurhan handles information in the Merge Kitchen mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

We do not directly collect any personal information. The App saves your current game level locally on your device using localStorage. This data never leaves your device.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect device identifiers to serve ads. See Google's policy at: https://policies.google.com/privacy

3. App Tracking Transparency

On iOS 14.5+, the App requests ATT permission before accessing any advertising identifier. If declined, only non-personalized ads are shown.

4. Data Retention

We do not store personal data on our servers. Local game progress can be cleared by deleting the App.

5. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

6. Contact

Questions? Email: enginkurhan@icloud.com
